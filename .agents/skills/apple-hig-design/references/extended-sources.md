# Extended Apple Design Sources

Source-map baseline: **2026-10-25**, as recorded in the supplied archive: **26 selected WWDC sessions** from [Design videos](https://developer.apple.com/videos/design/) and **44 articles** from [Apple Design updates](https://developer.apple.com/design/whats-new/). The archive reports inspecting video descriptions for routing and checking link availability; this import does not independently recertify those checks. A reading-map entry is not a locally synthesized recommendation.

This is a selection, not an exhaustive catalog. For further discovery, use [Design videos](https://developer.apple.com/videos/design/), [Accessibility and Inclusion videos](https://developer.apple.com/videos/accessibility-inclusion/), and [the Design Pathway](https://developer.apple.com/design/get-started/).

## How to use these sources

Use current HIG for present recommendations and WWDC for explanations and platform evolution. For a material claim, read the complete relevant transcript passage and its surrounding qualification. Older sessions may show obsolete controls. Behind the Design interviews illustrate individual products and teams, not universal requirements.

## Articles with transferable lessons

These case-study lessons are not requirements to reproduce a feature or workflow.

- [Meet the prototypers](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=97dgimaa): Prototype to answer a specific uncertainty with the least work needed. Compare alternatives, show them to people, and iterate from feedback; polish alone is not the objective.
- [Behind the Design: Oko](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=58c4urmu): Real-world testing with the intended audience changed orientation and feedback choices. A simple interface and complementary visual, audio, and haptic feedback served the actual task.
- [Behind the Design: Crouton](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=9x75y43e): Keep the active step, ingredients, and measurements together so people can return to the task. Add capabilities without abandoning the product's core intention.
- [Behind the Design: Flighty](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=970ncww4): Prioritize essential information in stressful contexts and anticipate lost connectivity. Familiar real-world information conventions can reduce interpretation effort.
- [Behind the Design: Any Distance](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=uiiopcl8): An inclusive audience and deliberate privacy controls can coexist with expressive data presentation. The case is not permission to add social sharing.
- [Behind the Design: Procreate](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=e409h6ja): Accessibility can deepen the main creative task. Iterative motion filtering illustrates solving an underlying user need while preserving a low barrier to entry.
